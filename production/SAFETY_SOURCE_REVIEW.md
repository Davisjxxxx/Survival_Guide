[Wilderness Medical Society 2024 guideline, field treatment scenarios and Table 1](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10806032231222359) |
| B012 P09–P20 | Regional winter advice supports protection from exposure. Minnesota land-use rules constrain collection and campsite alteration. The references do not establish the stability of an illustrated roof, a safe universal fire distance or a ventilation specification for a snow enclosure. Each remains open. [NWS winter storm advice](https://www.weather.gov/safety/winter-during) · [Minnesota DNR dispersed camping](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-forests/dispersed-camping.html) · [CDC carbon monoxide](https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html) |

[DNR thickness table and stationary loads](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/safety/ice/thickness.html) · [DNR measurement methods](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/safety/ice/check-ice-thickness.html) · [Minnesota angling regulations](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/regulations/fishing/index.html) |
