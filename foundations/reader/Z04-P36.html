Z04-P36 · Water · Adapted historical material

Operate pump, squeeze and gravity filters without cross-contaminating outlets

Keep raw intake hoses, wet dirty bags and hands physically isolated from clean spouts and potable bottles.

When it applies · Field operation of portable hand-pump, squeeze-pouch or gravity-bag water filters.

Equipment · Complete filter assembly with marked raw and clean lines, separate storage bags and clean potable bottle.

Actions

  1. Lay out the raw intake hose or dirty reservoir on one side and the clean outlet spout and open potable bottle on a separate clean surface.[W04, W06, ED]
  2. Hold the dirty squeeze bag or pump body without letting raw water drip down the outside of the bottle threads onto the clean nozzle or cap.[W06, ED]
  3. Store the raw hose and dirty bag inside their own sealed pouch apart from the clean outlet hose and drinking bottles after use.[W06, ED]

Failure recognition · Letting a wet dirty squeeze pouch drip raw stream water over the rim of the clean bottle while squeezing into the opening.[W06, ED]

Verify · Confirm the clean outlet never touches raw water, dirty bags, ground soil or unwashed fingers during filtering and packing.[W06, ED]

Stop or change course · If raw water splashes onto the clean outlet or inside the finished bottle, empty or re-treat the batch and clean the contact surface before drinking.[W06, W04, ED]
