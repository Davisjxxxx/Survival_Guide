Z14-P49 · Food production and preservation · New manuscript

Inspect Mason canning jars, sealing rims, and single-use two-piece lids

Prevent mid-process glass breakage, hidden chipped-rim seal failures, and reusable-lid spoilage.

When it applies · Preparing jars and lids prior to every boiling-water or pressure-canning batch.

Equipment · True tempered Mason-type home canning jars (standard/wide-mouth), NEW flat plastisol sealing lids, and unrusted intact metal screw bands.

Actions

  1. Use ONLY true tempered home canning jars (Mason/Ball/Kerr style) designed for thermal shock—NEVER reuse commercial single-use mayonnaise, pasta-sauce, or peanut-butter glass jars, which crack in pressure canners and fail to match two-piece lid sealing rims.[P09]
  2. Run a clean fingertip slowly around the ENTIRE glass lip of every jar and inspect against bright light for hairline cracks, nicks, or tiny chips—even a pinhead rim nick breaks the vacuum seal permanently; discard chipped jars from canning service.[P09]
  3. Always use BRAND-NEW flat metal sealing lids for every single canning batch (the sealing compound compresses permanently once processed and CANNOT be safely reused for canning), paired with clean, unbent, rust-free reusable screw bands.[P09]

Failure recognition · Reusing last year's flat sealing lids or canning salsa in thin recycled commercial spaghetti-sauce jars that crack in the canner.[P09]

Verify · Confirm true Mason jars checked fingertip-smooth for nicks/cracks, brand-new flat lids prepared per manufacturer directions, and clean unbent rings.[P09]

Stop or change course · Chipped jar rims, hairline glass cracks, dented flat lids, or heavily rusted bands must be discarded before filling jars.[P09]
