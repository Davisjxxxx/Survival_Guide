Z04-P42 · Water · New manuscript

Sequence clarification, filtration and chemical or UV disinfection

Combine physical clarification, pore filtration and secondary disinfection in the right order to close single-method gaps.

When it applies · Untreated surface water where turbidity, parasites, bacteria and viruses may coexist and chemical hazards are excluded.

Equipment · Pre-filter cloth or settling vessel, cyst-rated filter and matched chemical disinfectant or UV purifier.

Actions

  1. Clarify turbid water first by settling or cloth straining so sediment does not clog the fine filter cartridge or block UV light.[W08, W24, W11]
  2. Pass clarified water through a cyst- and bacteria-rated filter into a clean vessel to remove protozoa (Giardia, Cryptosporidium) and particulate shielding.[W04, W19, W20]
  3. Apply the secondary barrier—label-directed chemical disinfection or rated UV cycle—to inactivate viruses and any bacteria not caught by the filter, keeping threads clean.[W04, W05, W11]

Failure recognition · Running UV or bleach before settling cloudy water, or filtering after disinfection through a dirty cartridge that reintroduces microbes.[W08, W11, W04]

Verify · Confirm each barrier ran in strict order (screen -> clarify -> filter -> disinfect) with no raw-side contact after the final step.[W04, W08, W06, ED]

Stop or change course · If any required barrier fails or chemical contamination is suspected, switch to boiling (if chemically safe) or seek an alternate potable source.[W01, W02, W04]
