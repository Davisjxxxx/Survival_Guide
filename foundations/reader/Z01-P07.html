Z01-P07 · Survival fundamentals · Adapted historical material

Give rescuers a usable location

Report what you know and identify uncertainty explicitly.

When it applies · Calling for rescue at an outdoor emergency in the United States.

Equipment · Phone or suitable communicator, offline map and light if available.

Actions

  1. Give the site or park name plus trailhead, road, junction or other verified landmark. If sending coordinates, read the format shown by the device.[T06, ED]
  2. State how many people are involved, whether anyone is injured and what nearby hazards affect access.[T06, T02]
  3. Specify whether you will wait or must move. Follow dispatcher directions and communicate changes if you can.[T06, T02]

Verify · Ask whether your location was received and which approach the responder wants you to use, if any.[ED]

Stop or change course · Avoid moving solely to improve service if the route is uncertain or hazardous.[T06, ED]
