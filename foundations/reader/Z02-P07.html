Z02-P07 · Shelter and housing · Adapted historical material

Keep insulation dry across a stop

Prevent sweat, wet gear and precipitation from soaking rest layers.

When it applies · Cold travel where activity alternates with standing or resting.

Equipment · Dry spare insulation, weather shell and pack protection for dry items.

Actions

  1. Adjust layers before heavy exertion and use ventilation where weather permits. Avoid getting clothing wet with sweat.[E10]
  2. Before a stop, find wind protection and add the reserved dry layer. Separate wet items from dry insulation.[E05, E10, ED]
  3. Check companions for unusual fumbles, slurred speech or confusion as exposure continues.[E06]

Verify · The rest layer is dry and easy to reach, and the group can stop without standing in exposed wind.[E05, ED]

Stop or change course · Suspected hypothermia needs medical help and protection against further cooling.[E06, T03]
