Z01-P02 · Survival fundamentals · Adapted historical material

Assess before you move

Use location, daylight and group condition to decide whether travel makes sense.

When it applies · A stopped group with a potentially safe route and enough time to assess.

Equipment · Offline map or paper map, watch, light and weather information if available.

Actions

  1. Inventory the group, the weather, the remaining daylight and the last place you can identify confidently.[E01, E02, ED]
  2. Compare what you see with a mapped trail, junction or other reliable feature. Record the last verified place if your current location is uncertain.[T06, E01]
  3. Move only when the group can name a safe next checkpoint and is able to reach it. Keep current conditions and the slowest member in the decision.[T06, ED]

Verify · Before stepping off, someone can point to the next checkpoint and explain how the party will know it has reached it.[ED]

Stop or change course · Weather, terrain or group condition can invalidate a previously reasonable route. Halt if the next checkpoint becomes uncertain.[E01, T06]
