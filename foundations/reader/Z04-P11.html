Z04-P11 · Water

Check whether a UV device applies

Prevent misuse of portable UV disinfection in turbid water or an unsupported batch.

When it applies · Clear water from a chemically acceptable source and a working UV device rated for the intended use.

Equipment · Documented UV device, reliable power, suitable vessel and matched volume.

Actions

  1. Check the manufacturer's supported water clarity, volume and operating conditions. Suspended particles can reduce UV exposure.[W11]
  2. Follow the complete device cycle and any mixing instructions. Keep raw water and utensils apart from treated water.[W11, W06, ED]
  3. If the water remains cloudy, the device fails or power is unreliable, use a different supported treatment or known-safe supply.[W11, W02, W09]

Verify · Confirm a completed cycle at the volume and clarity the device specifies.[W11, ED]

Stop or change course · A UV device cannot resolve a suspected chemical contamination hazard.[W01]
