Z03-P24 · Fire and ignition · Adapted historical material

Understand ferrocerium rod scraping mechanics and spark direction

Understand how a sharp striker shaves burning pyrophoric ferrocerium particles cleanly into prepared fine tinder.

When it applies · Supervised practice or lawful outdoor ignition using a commercial ferrocerium rod and dedicated striker.

Equipment · Ferrocerium rod, sharp 90-degree striker (not a knife's sharp cutting edge), receptive fine dry tinder and fire-control water.

Actions

  1. Identify the rod, compatible striker and suitable dry tinder from the model instructions. Inspect tool control cold before any authorized live use.[C03, C11, ED]
  2. Ferrocerium and traditional flint-and-steel produce hot particles from different materials. Follow the product instructions for any live training.[C10, ED]
  3. Avoid striking with a knife's cutting edge, which dulls the blade and risks severe hand lacerations if the blade slips toward fingers or legs.[T01, ED]

Failure recognition · Waving a ferro rod wildly in midair above coarse sticks, scattering sparks harmlessly away from the tinder or slicing a hand with a knife blade.[C03, T01, ED]

Verify · In a cold dry-run position check, confirm stable braced tip placement, sharp square striker spine and fine fibrous tinder directly in the spark path.[C03, C11, ED]

Stop or change course · Keep live sparks strictly on authorized cleared sites with water ready; if cold numb hands prevent controlled scraping, use a lighter, matches or no-fire warmth.[C01, C02, C08, E06]
