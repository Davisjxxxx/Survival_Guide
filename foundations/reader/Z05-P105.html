Giant & Gem-Studded Puffballs (Calvatia & Lycoperdon) vs Amanita Eggs & Earthballs

Document diagnostic traits of Calvatia gigantea and Lycoperdon perlatum and contrasting lookalikes under permanent consumption HOLD.

When it applies · Minnesota meadows, pastures, woods; Late Summer-Autumn (Vertical cutaway of firm white interior).

Equipment · Mycology key, cm ruler, black-and-white spore card, paper bag.

Actions

  1. Verify Calvatia gigantea and Lycoperdon perlatum ((Batsch) Lloyd and Pers.; syn. Meadow & woodland puffballs) via Bell Museum Fungarium and MyCoPortal: Round-to-pear-shaped white-to-tan fruiting body sliced cleanly top-to-bottom revealing 100% uniform, firm, pure-white marshmallow-like interior flesh with ZERO hidden outline of cap, gills, or stem.[B05, B06, B07, B24]
  2. Exclude lookalikes (Immature Amanita egg buttons (LETHAL — vertical slice reveals embryonic cap or gill or stem silhouette inside outer universal veil!), Scleroderma citrinum (toxic pigskin earthball — thick leathery rind, dark purple-black interior)); reject on: ANY faint internal outline of gills or stem (LETHAL Amanita egg!); dark purplish or yellow or olive interior (earthball or maturing puffball spores).[B05, B06, B07, B24, B08]
  3. Separate recognition from eating (Slicing EVERY puffball candidate top-to-bottom is mandatory to catch lethal Amanita eggs; consumption strictly WITHHELD); obey non-SNA and clean-site rules under WITHHELD_HOLD.[B05, B24]

Failure recognition · Single-trait guessing or common names confuse Calvatia gigantea and Lycoperdon perlatum with Immature Amanita egg buttons, risking poisoning.[B03, B06, B24]

Verify · Binomial, multi-trait key match, lookalike exclusions, season and habitat, and WITHHELD_HOLD status are logged.[B05, B24]

Stop or change course · Stop if any key trait is missing or ambiguous; call Minnesota Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) or 911 on suspected ingestion.[B09, B24]
