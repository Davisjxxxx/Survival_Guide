Herbaceous Greens Multi-Character Lookalike Stop Matrix

Document diagnostic traits of Apiaceae and Solanaceae and Apocynaceae and contrasting lookalikes under permanent consumption HOLD.

When it applies · All Minnesota meadows, woods, wetlands, and edges; Spring-Autumn (Vegetative rosette through fruiting).

Equipment · Regional references, accession information and non-contact photographs or notes, where safe.

Actions

  1. Compare the working name Apiaceae and Solanaceae and Apocynaceae (Cross-family synthesis; syn. High-risk herbaceous lookalike groups) against a current regional key and accession. Unverified draft cues: Synthesizes the four highest-risk herbaceous exclusion cues: compound umbels, milky latex sap, glossy unpowdered nightshade foliage, and smooth non-flowering wetland fans.[B10, B11, B14, B20, B24]
  2. Use the comparisons and draft traits in the taxonomic register for qualified regional review. Do not use any single feature, comparison, smell or image to rule out a toxic species.[B02, B20, B24]
  3. Separate recognition from eating (Zero herbaceous wild greens are cleared for consumption without expert review); keep WITHHELD_HOLD and check site and species protections before any specialist fieldwork.[B19, B21, B24]

Failure recognition · Single-trait guessing or common names confuse Apiaceae and Solanaceae and Apocynaceae with Cicuta maculata, risking poisoning.[B03, B10, B24]

Verify · Keep the identification provisional until the voucher, region, life stage and independent diagnostic characters are reviewed by the qualified specialist.[B02, B20]

Stop or change course · Keep consumption on WITHHELD_HOLD. For suspected ingestion call U.S. Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222 promptly. Call 911 for collapse, seizure, breathing trouble or inability to wake.[B09, M15]
