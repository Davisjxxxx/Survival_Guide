Emergency Scene Safety and STOP Triad Cross-Reference

Index immediate scene-safety, lost-person STOP, and 911 escalation gates across Z01, Z10, and Z11 without dropping upstream limits.

When it applies · First 60 seconds of any backcountry, weather, or trauma emergency.

Equipment · Full offline reader (Z01, Z10, Z11), primary zone cards, and local emergency and agency references.

Actions

  1. Pause on stable ground (Z01-P01, Z11-P43) to scan active traffic, water, ice, fire, electrical, or overhead-snag hazards before approaching a casualty or moving further.[A01, T01]
  2. Activate 911 or emergency SOS early (Z01-P16, Z10-P01) while establishing shelter, bleeding control, or rescue signaling rather than delaying for uncertain self-rescue.[A01, A09, M01]
  3. Defer immediately to the full upstream Z01, Z10, or Z11 card for scene-specific exclusions; never treat a condensed atlas summary as standalone clinical clearance.[A01, A10]

Failure recognition · Rushing into an active water, ice, gas, electrical, or wildlife hazard creates a second casualty and delays rescue.[A01, T01]

Verify · Scene hazards are checked, exact upstream Z01, Z10, Z11 card IDs are located, and emergency help is notified early.[A01, A09]

Stop or change course · Stop advancing into any unverified hazard zone and hold on safe ground until qualified responders or verified conditions permit movement.[A01, T01]
