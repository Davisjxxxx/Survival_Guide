B013 · MINNESOTA ICE SAFETY · P03 · MAP / AVOID

FIND THE WEAK ZONES

Current and transition areas can undermine ice locally

STAGED SCENE • NOT A RELIABLE ICE-CONDITION IDENTIFICATION PHOTO
Water beneath the surface is moving. Ice can thin rapidly where that water flows, warms or meets a structure.

HOW THE DECISION OR ACTION WORKS

  1. Locate inflows and outflows — Mark creeks, springs, outlets, culverts and drainage channels.
  2. Locate bottlenecks — Avoid narrows, bridges and channels with currents.
  3. Inspect edges from shore — Shore ice, reeds and overflow can conceal weak transitions.
  4. Plan without crossing — Route on stable ground; do not approach open leads or pressure ridges.

CHECK BEFORE CONTINUING

  • Potential current zones are excluded from the route.
  • Map and visible terrain are reconciled before movement.

WRONG SIGNAL → CORRECTION

One thickness measurementIce can differ sharply nearby.
Bridge or culvert shortcutFlowing water can thin ice unexpectedly.
A pressure ridge looks solidBreaks and refrozen cracks are unstable transition areas.
STOP CONDITION: If ice strength cannot be verified, stay off. If someone has fallen through, summon emergency responders immediately.
