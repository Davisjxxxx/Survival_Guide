B014 · NORTH WOODS NAVIGATION · P16 · SIGNALING / FOREST CANOPY

MAKE YOUR POSITION FINDABLE

Searchers cannot always see someone beneath dense forest canopy.

SCENARIO · You are safe enough to remain in place, but the forest conceals you from a road or aircraft.
Options from least equipment
NO GEAR: Use your voice, bright clothing or deliberate movement that does not put you in danger.
IMPROVISED: Arrange contrasting materials on safe open ground without damaging the environment.
EQUIPPED: Use a whistle, signaling mirror, light or personal locator beacon as appropriate.
Four-action sequence
  1. CALL — Use 911 or an emergency communicator if available; report your location and last known route.
  2. SIGNAL — Use a whistle, visible clothing or a light from stable ground.
  3. STAY TOGETHER — Keep the group at the reported location unless a life threat requires movement.
  4. REPEAT CAREFULLY — Signal when responders are near; conserve battery and energy while awaiting help.

Check before continuing

  • Can a dispatcher describe your actual position?
  • Does a signal risk pushing someone onto water, roads or unstable ground?
  • Can the group remain together and visible?

Wrong signal → correction

Run toward every sound → Unknown direction can disperse the group.
Build a signal fire anywhere → Fire can cause wildfire or injury; avoid without safe legal conditions and skill.
STOP / ABORT CONDITION: Do not travel toward distant voices or aircraft unless responders give a safe, verified instruction.
