Be Found: Signal, Stay or Move

In the North Woods, a safe known location and repeatable signal are often more useful than wandering.

AT A GLANCE

  • Contact emergency services when possible.
  • If lost, generally stay at a safe location unless facing immediate danger.
  • Be seen and heard without exposing yourself to worse conditions.
STAY FINDABLE • SHARE LOCATION • AVOID NEW HAZARDS
1

Stop and locate

Identify last-known point, trail, lake, road and any reliable coordinates. Avoid guessing where a route goes.

2

Call for help

Call 911 if possible; provide coordinates, landmarks, clothing colors, people, injuries and planned route.

3

Make a visible marker

Use contrasting cloth or arranged materials on safe open ground. Signal from shelter when possible; repeat sound and light signals if you have them.

4

Protect the position

Stay out of unsafe ice, drainage, dead timber and whiteout. Continue monitoring exposure and rescue conditions.

DECISION DIAGRAM
  1. Remain in a safe known location unless immediate danger requires moving.
  2. Create a large, contrasting signal without separating from shelter or taking unsafe routes.
  3. Tell responders coordinates or last-known location, landmarks and injuries; stay findable.
NO EQUIPMENT → IMPROVISED → EQUIPPED

NO EQUIPMENT

Use deliberate ground contrast in a safe clearing; repeated audible calls if rescuers may be nearby.

IMPROVISED

Bright clothing, salvage panels, reflective metal or a harmless marker near shelter.

EQUIPPED

Phone, satellite messenger, whistle, strobe or GPS coordinates; test coverage but never rely on it alone.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Leaving a vehicle or known trail during whiteoutReturn to a safe established point if possible; do not undertake blind travel.
Making a signal too far from shelterRelocate the signal within safe line of sight; protect heat and energy.
Lighting a signal fire in unsafe conditionsDo not create a wildfire or enclosed smoke hazard; use visual or audible alternatives.
STOP / ESCALATE: MOVE only to escape immediate danger or reach a reliably known nearby refuge by a safe route. Otherwise communicate, signal, shelter, and remain findable.
