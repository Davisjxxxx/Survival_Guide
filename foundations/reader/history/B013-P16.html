B013 · MINNESOTA ICE SAFETY · P16 · ZERO-EQUIPMENT LIMITS

NO TOOLS? DO NOT MAKE AN ICE HOLE

Ice fishing is not a viable survival shortcut without safe access and proper tools.

ORIGINAL DIAGRAM · METHODS SHOWN AS PROHIBITED CHOICES
This guide recommends staying on land when you lack safe access, ice assessment equipment or rescue equipment. It does not recommend improvised hole-making.

FIELD ACTION · NO GEAR FIRST

1
No gear: do not attempt ice fishingDo not walk onto unknown ice. Prioritize protection from wind, dry insulation, finding shelter and contacting help.
2
Improvised: reject hole-making shortcutsDo not stomp, strike, heat, chip or melt a fishing hole in natural ice with improvised materials.
3
If shore access existsOnly fish from stable ground into open water if that is physically possible and lawful. Fishing is secondary to safety.
4
Equipped: use a compliant planAngling gear, a properly used auger and appropriate ice safety equipment do not eliminate ice hazards; verify conditions before travel.

VISUAL DECISION / METHOD

  1. No gear: do not attempt ice fishing — Do not walk onto unknown ice. Prioritize protection from wind, dry insulation, finding shelter and contacting help.
  2. Improvised: reject hole-making shortcuts — Do not stomp, strike, heat, chip or melt a fishing hole in natural ice with improvised materials.
  3. If shore access exists — Only fish from stable ground into open water if that is physically possible and lawful. Fishing is secondary to safety.
  4. Equipped: use a compliant plan — Angling gear, a properly used auger and appropriate ice safety equipment do not eliminate ice hazards; verify conditions before travel.

CHECK BEFORE CONTINUING

  • No one is standing or crouching on uncertain ice.
  • A legal fishing method and license/exemption are confirmed before angling.
  • No self-made rescue hazard is left on the ice.

WRONG SIGNAL → CORRECTION

A rock can break thin iceBreaking a surface also creates a fall hazard: abandon the idea.
Build a fire to melt a holeDo not compromise ice integrity or create a burning hazard.
Fish will solve starvation quicklyAcquiring calories may take time and increase cold exposure; prioritize rescue.
STOP: Do not teach improvised through-ice fishing as a validated zero-equipment technique.
