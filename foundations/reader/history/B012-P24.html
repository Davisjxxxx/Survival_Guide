Hypothermia: Recognize & Respond

Mental status matters more than a guessed temperature. Confusion or abnormal drowsiness is an emergency.

AT A GLANCE

  • Cold + wet can cause hypothermia above freezing.
  • Clumsy hands, slurred speech, confusion and drowsiness are warnings.
  • Call 911 for confusion, reduced alertness or collapse.
RECOGNIZE • STOP HEAT LOSS • GET HELP
1

Recognize the change

Notice fumbling, stumbling, unusual behavior, confused or mumbled speech.

2

Call for help

Activate 911 for suspected serious hypothermia; report location and responsiveness.

3

Protect from further cold

Handle an impaired person gently; shelter from wind, insulate underneath, cover head/neck, leave face unobstructed.

4

Warm and monitor

Replace wet clothing when sheltered with dry insulation ready. Warm torso using safe wrapped sources if available; observe breathing and alertness.

DECISION DIAGRAM
  1. Alert and shivering: shelter, insulate, reassess.
  2. Confused, slurred or markedly impaired: call 911, handle gently and warm torso.
  3. Unresponsive: call 911, assess breathing and follow dispatcher-guided CPR.
NO EQUIPMENT → IMPROVISED → EQUIPPED

NO EQUIPMENT

Use safe terrain to block wind; dry natural insulation beneath and around without pressing wet material against skin.

IMPROVISED

Use dry clothing, bags, salvage or blankets as insulation; keep a wind/water barrier outside dry layers.

EQUIPPED

Call rescue; follow approved warming-device instructions, put barrier between heat and skin, monitor for burns.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Someone becomes confused or cannot follow instructionsTreat as medical emergency: call 911, minimize movement, protect heat.
Person is drowsy or cannot swallow safelyDo not give drinks or food; monitor breathing and follow emergency instructions.
Hot rocks, open flame or vigorous limb rubbingDo not use direct heat or massage; risk burns and dangerous shifts in blood flow.
STOP / ESCALATE: If unconscious or not breathing normally: call 911, follow dispatcher-guided CPR/AED instructions. Do not assume a profoundly cold person is beyond help.
