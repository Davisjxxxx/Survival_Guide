P17 | B012 WINTER SURVIVAL

Earth / stone shelter: do not improvise a roof

A winter pit does not guarantee warmth or safety

CONCEPT / CUTAWAY

Original schematic. Not a measured or field-tested structure.

Materials and upgrades

No gear: natural terrain windbreak and insulated bedding.

Improvised: existing safe building or recovered shelter; no pit roof.

Equipped: engineered, evaluated long-term construction only.

ACTION / DECISION SEQUENCE

  1. Reject unsupported excavation beneath frozen soil, boulders or roots.
  2. Assess trapped water, groundwater, frost movement and collapse before considering any excavation.
  3. Never construct an unvented hearth or fire pit inside a cavity or primitive enclosure.
  4. Prefer safer above-ground or existing engineered shelter if available.
FAILURE: Excavation walls slough, stone supports shift, water collects, or a pit becomes difficult to exit.
CHANGE COURSE: Stop the excavation and move to stable above-ground location. Use dry insulation and known-safe shelter without a fire or heater in the excavation.

LIMIT: No soil strength, rock stability, roof loads, or drainage are established. A covered pit is not approved as an improvised winter shelter.
