Winter Failure Atlas: Notice & Correct

Do not repair a low-priority problem while a cold injury or unsafe structure is becoming life-threatening.

AT A GLANCE

  • Act on immediate injury and exposure first.
  • Repair conditions, not just the visible symptom.
  • A safer alternative is often better than more labor.
SYMPTOM → LIKELY ISSUE → STOP / CORRECTION
1

Cold person acts differently

New confusion, slurring or collapse: call 911; protect heat and handle gently.

2

Sleeping area stays wet

Wet leaves and ground contact: move to a drier site, replace wet layers, improve loft.

3

Shelter or fire fails

If supports sag or roof loads look unstable, exit. If fire attempts are exhausting, stop and prioritize shelter.

4

Travel or water choices worsen

No uncertain ice crossings, no untreated water assumed safe, no travel through whiteout to fetch optional resources.

DECISION DIAGRAM
  1. Wet bed: move to drier ground and restore dry lofted insulation.
  2. Sagging roof: leave the unstable shelter.
  3. Fire failure: conserve energy and switch to shelter/dryness.
  4. Confusion: call emergency services and protect body heat.
  5. Numb waxy skin: protect from additional freezing; do not rub.
  6. Uncertain ice or whiteout: do not cross or travel blindly.
NO EQUIPMENT → IMPROVISED → EQUIPPED

NO EQUIPMENT

Change site, reduce wind, replace wet debris, protect head and extremities, conserve energy.

IMPROVISED

Use salvaged dry cloth, wind sheet, supported frame pieces and high-contrast signal markers.

EQUIPPED

Use rescue communications, winter-rated insulation, rated water treatment and trained structural assistance.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Heat loss persistsCheck moisture, wet bedding, wind leaks and missing ground insulation.
Shelter roof begins saggingGet out and abandon unsupported overhead load; do not sleep beneath it.
Numb fingers or behavioral changesTreat cold injury and seek medical help, not more firewood.
Water looks clear after sand filteringStill not necessarily disinfected; choose a validated treatment or another source.
STOP / ESCALATE: Stop immediately for altered mental status, collapse risk, unsafe ice, unvented combustion or escalating inability to stay warm.
