Z02-P05 · Shelter and housing

Leave a drainage channel early

Recognize where moving water will concentrate before precipitation reaches the site.

When it applies · Campsites and travel near creeks, gullies, marsh edges and valley bottoms.

Equipment · Map, weather alerts and a light if available.

Actions

  1. Look for connected low ground and a clear route to higher ground. Study upstream conditions as part of the decision.[E04]
  2. Select a higher, stable site away from streambeds and gullies. Be ready to move if warnings, rising water or new runoff appear.[E04]
  3. Leave early by a safe route to higher ground. Follow local emergency directions.[E04, T07]

Verify · Someone in the group can identify both the hazard path and the exit without entering the drainage.[ED]

Stop or change course · Never cross flooded routes to retrieve gear or reach the planned exit.[T07]
