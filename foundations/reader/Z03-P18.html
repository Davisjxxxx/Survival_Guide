Z03-P18 · Fire and ignition · Adapted historical material

Stage fuel lay geometry for steady airflow without smothering

Arrange kindling and fuel over tinder so rising heat preheats upper sticks while cool air feeds the base.

When it applies · Building a small authorized outdoor campfire in a cleared mineral-soil ring.

Equipment · Staged dry tinder, progressive kindling sizes, cleared fire ring, water and stirring tool.

Actions

  1. Place a dry wood or mineral base under tinder on damp ground and arrange fine kindling loosely above with an open windward air gap so flames climb cleanly upward.[C03, C02, ED]
  2. Leave space between sticks for oxygen draw—air feeds from underneath while heat rises through stacked kindling to dry and ignite the next size up.[C03, ED]
  3. Add medium fuel gradually only after kindling burns steadily, keeping all wood ends completely inside the fire ring perimeter.[C02, C03, C17]

Failure recognition · Dropping heavy split logs straight onto a fragile match flame, crushing air channels and creating thick eye-stinging smoke without sustained heat.[C03, C02]

Verify · Confirm clear bottom airflow channel, gradual stick-size progression and all fuel contained strictly inside the cleared ring.[C02, C03, ED]

Stop or change course · If gusts blow flames sideways out of the ring or fuel shifts unpredictably, stop adding wood and drown the fire completely.[C02, C04, C18]

Limitations: Site clearances vary by fuel load, slope, and wind; no universal safe radius fits all terrain. · Conflicting evidence: None; aligns with USFS-FIRE.

C04 · USDA Forest Service / Smokey Bear · Smokey Bear’s Campfire Safety Guide (agency PDF)
Section: Maintain and extinguish a campfire · Conditions: Occupied campfire, water and tools available
Support: Stay with the fire. Drown, stir and add water again until cool. Leaving visible embers or merely covering them is unsafe.
Limitations: Deep duff or peat soils can hold smoldering embers underground even after surface ash cools. · Conflicting evidence: None; prohibits dirt-only burial as primary extinguishment.
Editorial note: Enforces drown-stir-feel-cold cycle before site departure. · Specialist reviewer: Fire prevention and land management

C17 · NPS · Leave No Trace Seven Principles (current web edition)
Section: Principle 5 > Minimize Campfire Impacts · Conditions: Permitted backcountry sites where wood fires are allowed
Support: Where fires are permitted, keep fires small, use only dead and downed wood gathered lawfully, burn wood completely to ash, and use a camp stove where wood is scarce or impacts are high.
Limitations: Some parks prohibit all wood gathering or open fires regardless of deadfall availability. · Conflicting evidence: None; balances lawful fuel collection with habitat protection.
Editorial note: Guides lawful fuel gathering and small-fire thermal management. · Specialist reviewer: Land management and fire prevention
