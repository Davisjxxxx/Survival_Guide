Sugar Maple and Red Maple (Acer saccharum & A. rubrum) Profile

Document diagnostic traits of Acer saccharum and Acer rubrum and contrasting lookalikes under permanent consumption HOLD.

When it applies · Hardwood forests across Minnesota; Year-round (Opposite buds, lobed leaves & samaras).

Equipment · Regional references, accession information and non-contact photographs or notes, where safe.

Actions

  1. Compare the working name Acer saccharum and Acer rubrum (Marshall and L.; syn. Hard & soft Minnesota maples) against a current regional key and accession. Unverified draft cues: Strictly opposite twigs and buds; palmate five-lobed leaves (U-shaped smooth sinuses in sugar maple; saw-toothed V-sinuses in red maple); paired winged samara fruits.[B01, B16, B20, B23]
  2. Use the comparisons and draft traits in the taxonomic register for qualified regional review. Do not use any single feature, comparison, smell or image to rule out a toxic species.[B02, B20, B24]
  3. Separate recognition from eating (Live tree tapping is prohibited in parks or SNAs; sap spoils rapidly if unrefrigerated; consumption withheld); keep WITHHELD_HOLD and check site and species protections before any specialist fieldwork.[B19, B21, B24]

Failure recognition · Single-trait guessing or common names confuse Acer saccharum and Acer rubrum with Acer negundo, risking poisoning.[B03, B10, B24]

Verify · Keep the identification provisional until the voucher, region, life stage and independent diagnostic characters are reviewed by the qualified specialist.[B02, B20]

Stop or change course · Keep consumption on WITHHELD_HOLD. For suspected ingestion call U.S. Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222 promptly. Call 911 for collapse, seizure, breathing trouble or inability to wake.[B09, M15]
