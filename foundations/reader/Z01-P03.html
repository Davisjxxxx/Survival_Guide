Z01-P03 · Survival fundamentals

Stay, signal or move

Make an explicit choice between staying findable and moving to a known safe position.

When it applies · Lost or overdue outdoor party. Immediate danger and known route are separate questions.

Equipment · Phone or emergency communicator, whistle, light, bright material and weather protection if available.

Actions

  1. Stop. If an immediate hazard threatens the spot, reach safer ground nearby only when you can identify a safe way there.[T06]
  2. Contact 911 when help is needed and service is available. Report last known position, landmarks, number of people, injuries and any planned movement.[T06, T02]
  3. If you cannot determine a safe route, stay together in a findable location, shelter from weather and prepare to signal when responders are nearby.[T06, E05]

Verify · Your location description gives responders a usable starting point. Mark a plan change in your message if you must move.[T06, ED]

Stop or change course · Never attempt an uncertain shortcut over water or ice to reach a signal or a road.[T06, ED]
