Z14-R01 · Food production and preservation · Reconstruction draft

Match preservation plans to tested recipes

Identify the exact tested process before preparing produce for shelf-stable storage.

Lineage · Preparedness primer reconstruction · Small Acreage Self-Reliance

When it applies · Household home canning. This card selects an authoritative recipe and gives no processing schedule.

Equipment · Validated recipe for the food, container size and equipment. Determine local elevation.

Actions

  1. Find a tested recipe for the exact food and preparation. Distinguish low-acid foods that require a tested pressure-canning process from foods with other tested processes.[F06]
  2. Match the jar size, ingredients and equipment to the recipe. Obtain its altitude adjustment before beginning.[F06, F07]
  3. If the recipe or equipment does not cover your ingredients, elevation or container, choose a different tested recipe or another approved preservation method.[F06, F07, ED]

Verify · The recipe citation, equipment, jar size and altitude instructions agree before processing.[F07, ED]

Stop or change course · Do not improvise processing time, pressure or ingredients to salvage an untested recipe.[F06, F07]
