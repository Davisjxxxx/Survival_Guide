Spotted Water Hemlock (Cicuta maculata): Hazard Recognition

Recognize a possible high-consequence plant hazard and avoid disturbing it.

When it applies · Suspect plant encounters in wet areas. Habitat does not establish identification.

Equipment · Regional references, accession information and non-contact photographs or notes, where safe.

Actions

  1. Treat a possible water hemlock encounter as an avoidance decision. A CDC case documents severe poisoning following ingestion. Field recognition requires a qualified regional identification.[B11, B02]
  2. Do not dig, cut, taste or handle a suspect plant to test its identity. Similar habitats or umbrella-shaped flowers cannot establish a safe identification.[B11, B20, B24]
  3. Keep consumption on WITHHELD_HOLD. Use the poison or emergency contact appropriate to the symptoms and circumstances.[B09, M15]

Failure recognition · Single-trait guessing or common names confuse Cicuta maculata with Wild carrot, risking poisoning.[B03, B10, B24]

Verify · Keep the identification provisional until the voucher, region, life stage and independent diagnostic characters are reviewed by the qualified specialist.[B02, B20]

Stop or change course · Keep consumption on WITHHELD_HOLD. For suspected ingestion call U.S. Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222 promptly. Call 911 for collapse, seizure, breathing trouble or inability to wake.[B09, M15]
