Z02-P08 · Shelter and housing · New manuscript

Choose shade and reduce exertion

Reduce heat load before the group becomes ill.

When it applies · Hot weather outdoors, especially exertion or long exposure.

Equipment · Accessible cool refuge, safe drinking water and a working way to call for help.

Actions

  1. Schedule exertion for cooler periods where possible. Use a shaded or air-conditioned rest location and take breaks before anyone becomes ill.[E08]
  2. Keep safe water accessible and check the group for changes in coordination, speech or behavior.[E08, T09, ED]
  3. If heat stroke is suspected, call 911 and start cooling promptly while following emergency directions.[T09]

Verify · Identify the nearest practical cool refuge and how the group will reach it before continuing.[E08, ED]

Stop or change course · Confusion, slurred speech or loss of consciousness in the heat is an emergency.[T09]
