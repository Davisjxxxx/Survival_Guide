Z01-P09 · Survival fundamentals · Adapted historical material

Prepare a communication backup

Keep a way to share essential information if a primary device fails.

When it applies · Trips without dependable voice or data service.

Equipment · Phone, paper map and trip plan, whistle and light. Add a suitable emergency communicator if available.

Actions

  1. Save the route, emergency numbers and trip information before leaving coverage. Carry a paper copy independent of the phone battery.[E01, E02, ED]
  2. Check how your equipment works before the trip and agree on an overdue trigger with a contact. A signal device helps nearby responders but cannot promise that someone knows your location.[E01, T06, ED]
  3. If no message can get through, avoid uncertain travel just to find signal. Prepare to be located using known landmarks, shelter and available signals.[T06, E05, ED]

Verify · Demonstrate one location report and one backup signal while the phone is disconnected.[ED]

Stop or change course · Do not assume responders were notified without confirmation. Use the next safe communication option in your trip plan.[T06, ED]
