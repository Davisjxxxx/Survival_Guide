## How a plate is accepted as a provisional editorial candidate

1. Source support is established before drawing. Card claims are read at their cited section; missing or misattributed support is recorded in `W2C_EVIDENCE_DECISIONS.csv`.
2. The drawing shows the physical relationship itself. `scripts/w2c_label_audit.py` lays every plate out at 720 px (the production vector-audit width) and fails on clipping, label collisions or labels crossing a panel edge; shape and leader collisions are still checked by eye. Computed plates are generated from a model and checked numerically: `scripts/build_w2c_knots.py` (rope crossing order, knot determinant, tail position) and `scripts/build_w2c_navigation.py` (dial, needle, neat-line and declination angles).
3. `scripts/verify_w2c_visual.py <batch> --axe <axe.min.js>` opens every page offline at 390 and 1280 px, runs axe-core, captures the page context, and prints tagged Letter and A4 proofs checked with the established proof inspection.
4. An AI visual inspection of the captures and print sheets is recorded in the ledger. It is not a qualified human review, and automated checks alone never qualify a plate.

The three plates A04 returned for reconstruction, and Z11-P32, which RT01-08 required revising, keep their A04 candidates in place under `visual/v02_technical/revised/`. `scripts/check_a04.py` accepts a different active figure for those rows only when a W2C record chains the A04 candidate hash to the W2C predecessor, revision and active reader figure, and, for an accepted plate, names the recorded finding (`supersession_basis`).

Atlas custody: the owner-pending Z16 atlas cards and figures stay byte-identical to A04 except inside the evidence panel, which may carry current evidence assessments (RT01-11). The compact layout is not promoted.

## Proof and package refresh policy (RT01-06)

Every entry in `foundations/proofs/tagged-print-qa.json` records what its PDF was printed from: a fingerprint of each card's printed body and embedded figure (`page_inputs`) and of the print stylesheets and proof template (`style_inputs`), computed by `scripts/proof_inputs.py`. `scripts/check_foundations.py` recomputes them and fails when a proof's inputs changed, unless `foundations/proofs/PROOF-CURRENCY.json` withholds that proof with exactly its changed pages. Zone and reconstruction proofs ship inside offline packages and are always reprinted (`print_foundations_tagged.py --editions Z14,...`), never withheld. The consolidated encyclopedia and foundations proofs may be withheld between milestones and are then not current; they are reprinted at milestones and at the end of the run. A valid byte hash alone no longer makes an older proof current.

Every batch commit rebuilds the reader, reprints the affected zone proofs, refreshes the offline ZIP packages and carries tagged Letter and A4 proofs of the changed pages in `visual/w2c_context/<batch>/`, so `scripts/check_foundations.py` passes at every commit. Milestone M2 (after RT01 and W2C-B06) reprinted all 37 tagged proofs.

`scripts/check_rt01.py` reruns the RT01 acceptance receipts recorded in `RT01_IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## Commands

```sh
PY=/tmp/sfe-production-env/bin/python
AXE=/tmp/sfe-accessibility/node_modules/axe-core/axe.min.js
$PY scripts/test_foundation_controls.py
$PY scripts/build_foundations.py
$PY scripts/w2c_register.py --write
(cd scripts && $PY verify_w2c_visual.py <batch> --axe $AXE)
$PY scripts/audit_foundation_vectors.py foundations /tmp/sfe-vector-qa --fail-layout
$PY scripts/print_foundations_tagged.py
$PY scripts/verify_a04_visual.py --axe $AXE
$PY scripts/check_foundations.py --write-package
$PY scripts/check_foundations.py
$PY scripts/check_a04.py
$PY scripts/check_w2c.py
$PY scripts/check_rt01.py
(cd scripts && $PY w2c_label_audit.py ../foundations/visual/w2c_technical/revised/*.svg)
```

The production browser is the system Google Chrome (155.0.8059.79 on this machine), which the scripts select with `which google-chrome`; the Python environment needs Playwright, PyMuPDF, Beautiful Soup and Pillow, and axe-core 4.14.1 supplies the accessibility engine.
