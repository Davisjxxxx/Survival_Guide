## Z05 identification and review

- Establish one record for each accepted taxon with accepted binomial, authority, useful synonyms, regional scope, date and voucher accession. Plant and fungal taxonomic references are separate. Link the specimen or curator record and corroborate identification traits with a regional key. An occurrence record documents the reported specimen and place. Source leads include the [Bell Museum Minnesota Biodiversity Atlas](https://www.bellmuseum.umn.edu/atlas/) and [Bell Museum specimen collections](https://bellatlas.umn.edu/collections/index.php).
- Require several independently documented characters and a contrasting lookalike table. State life stage, plant part, season and observable exclusions. For fungi, plan safe observational documentation of cap, underside, stem/base and substrate as applicable. Record measurement scale and site-level habitat. Stop identification when a required character is unavailable or inconsistent.
- Record authentic item provenance, rights, field conditions, voucher linkage and curator or expert ID. Identify context photography and conceptual diagrams explicitly. Require separately sourced and reviewed poisonous lookalikes, including local risk even if a global example seems familiar. Maintain poison-center review for delayed symptoms and communication guidance. [CDC Minnesota outbreak report](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6821a4.htm) · [CDC 2026 Amanita outbreak report](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/75/wr/mm7520a2.htm)
- Keep the instructional workflow focused on recognition and avoidance. If identification, contamination, preparation, person-specific risks, rights or expert assessment remain uncertain, record the resulting consumption status as withheld. Generated imagery and incomplete diagnostic photos support no consumption clearance.

Use the [Minnesota DNR regulations index](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/regulations/fishing/index.html) and [MN Department of Health waterbody-specific advisories](https://www.health.state.mn.us/communities/environment/fish/guidelines.html) as P17 leads. Recheck the actual applicable rules at the time of drafting, including local and tribal authority where applicable. Obtain independent wildlife, legal and welfare review for Z07.

For each sprint, run a small first batch through the focused evidence ledger, layout checks, representative render inspection, canonical build and tagged proof. Then continue the original allocation in bounded batches. Keep the draft, source, image rights, physiological and legal review status separate.
