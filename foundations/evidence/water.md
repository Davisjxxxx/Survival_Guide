# Water sourcing, treatment and contamination · evidence pack

Checked 2026-11-24. United States emergency, household, and backcountry contexts with Upper Midwest regional extensions. Use the [claim register](claims.tsv) W01–W30 for original agency locators, limits, conflicting evidence, and required specialist reviewers, and the [source register](sources.tsv) for dated primary references.

| Decision | Source-supported boundary | Editorial implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Select and screen a supply | Rain, clear surface water, snowmelt, and flooded private wells can carry pathogens or dissolved chemicals. Visual clarity never establishes potability. | Move chemical, algal-bloom, watershed, and advisory screening upstream of all treatment choices. |
| Reject chemical and algal hazards | Boiling and standard microbial disinfection do not remove fuel, heavy metals, nitrates, solvents, radionuclides, or cyanotoxins; boiling HAB water can concentrate toxins. | Hard-stop rejection cards for fuel/industrial runoff, agricultural/mine watersheds, harmful algal blooms (W15–W17), and flooded wells (W18). |
| Clarify turbidity | Suspended silt, clay, and organics shield microbes from UV and disinfectants and clog porous filters (W08, W24). | Depict settling and cloth pre-filtering strictly on the raw side before microbial treatment. |
| Boil | CDC/EPA emergency standard: 1 full minute at a rolling boil; 3 minutes above 6,500 ft (W02). | Require rolling-boil timer verification, clean-side cooling, and optional clean-container aeration (W27). |
| Filter, disinfect, and UV | Filters vary by pore/certification (W04, W19–W21); chlorine/iodine leave parasite gaps (Cryptosporidium, W20) and iodine has pregnancy/thyroid contraindications (W10); UV requires clear water and rated batch/cycle (W11); bleach requires verifying 5%–9% unscented concentration and >=30 min wait (W12). | Separate single-barrier limits from filter-plus-disinfect pairing; require freeze-protection for wet hollow-fiber/ceramic filters (W28). |
| Store, sanitize, and manage advisories | Food-grade tight-closing containers washed and sanitized (W06, W13), >=1 gal/person/day floor rotated every 6 months (W14); match exact boil-water vs do-not-drink vs do-not-use notices (W07) and hygiene boundaries (W29). | Maintain strict raw-vs-clean segregation, container compatibility limits, and fallback escalation when treatment fails. |

Independent drinking-water and public-health specialists must review cold-water contact times, certified filter claims, regional chemical-screening guidance, and container-sanitation procedures prior to field authorization.
