# Home utilities evidence pack (`utilities`, U01–U20)

The A02 [claim audit](../audit/a02/CLAIM_SUPPORT_AUDIT.csv) links exact sections, conditions, exclusions and specialist dependencies. The [technical findings](../audit/a02/TECHNICAL_FINDINGS.csv) record the changes made after P12–P15.

Grounds Zone `Z13` (Home Survival and Utilities — 70 pages, preserving labeled reconstruction starter `Z13-R03`) in authoritative primary guidance from FEMA/Ready.gov, CPSC, NFPA, US EPA (Private Wells & Septic), CDC, and Minnesota Department of Health.

- **Scope boundary:** Applicable to Upper Midwest and Minnesota residential conditions while documenting jurisdictional and regional trade-code limits.
- **Hard-stop exclusions:** Never authorizes generator backfeeding into wall/dryer outlets, indoor fuel-burning heating/generators, DIY gas-service restoration, or treating conceptual wiring/plumbing/combustion diagrams as licensed installation instructions.
- **Specialist hold:** Electrical/transfer switches, gas/HVAC heating, private well repair/disinfection, and septic systems remain escalated to licensed trades, fire safety, and environmental health (`SPECIALIST_REVIEW_REGISTER.csv` H04 and P12–P15 additions).
