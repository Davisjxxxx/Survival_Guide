# Food production and preservation evidence pack (`preservation`, P01–P20)

Grounds Zone `Z14` (Food Production and Preservation — 80 pages, preserving labeled reconstruction starter `Z14-R01`) in authoritative primary sources from USDA (Complete Guide to Home Canning), National Center for Home Food Preservation (UGA), University of Minnesota Extension, and USDA/FDA/CDC FoodSafety.gov.

- **Scope boundary:** Treats every tested preservation protocol (recipe, pH/acidification, piece size, headspace, jar size, pressure/method, time, altitude) as an indivisible complete system.
- **Hard-stop exclusions:** Prohibits oven canning, open-kettle canning, boiling-water canning of low-acid foods, untested recipe substitutions, and fabricated multi-decade safe shelf-life claims.
- **Specialist hold:** Requires qualified food-preservation process authority, food microbiology, and university Extension horticulture/food-safety sign-off (`SPECIALIST_REVIEW_REGISTER.csv` H17 and P12–P15 additions).
