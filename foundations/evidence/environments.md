# Extreme environments and environmental hazards · evidence pack

Checked 2026-11-24. Upper Midwest winter/ice plus desert, mountain, wetland, coastal and maritime hazard environments across the United States. Use `X01–X18` alongside `E04–E26`, `T03–T09` and `W15–W28` in the [claim register](claims.tsv).

| Environment | Source-supported boundary | Editorial implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Upper Midwest winter & ice | No ice is universally safe; thickness tables apply only to new clear solid ice and never guarantee crossing safety (X01, E12); cold-water immersion triggers rapid cold shock and swim failure before hypothermia (X02, X03); self-rescue and reach/throw witness rules carry extreme hazard (X18). | Reconcile frozen B012 (28 pp) and B013 (18 pp) without modifying original files; flag high-consequence immersion/rescue cards in the Astra escalation register. |
| Desert & extreme heat | Heat rapidly accelerates dehydration and vehicle-cabin lethality; dry washes flash-flood from distant rain; staying with a disabled road vehicle generally beats walking into extreme heat (X07, X08, E08). | Separate desert shade/hydration pacing from heat-stroke emergency cooling and dust-storm/flash-flood thresholds. |
| Mountain environments | High elevation (>8,000 ft) causes AMS, HAPE and HACE requiring descent and medical care (X09); afternoon thunderstorms, rapid cooling, avalanche slopes (30–45° + runout) and burn-scar debris flows govern mountain timing (X10, X11, E23). | Require early starts, treeline turnaround before storms, zero unvalidated snow-shelter ratings and immediate descent for severe altitude symptoms. |
| Wetlands | Hydric soils, quaking bogs, concealed channels, cold-wet immersion and stagnant water require cautious footing, foot care and strict water treatment (X12, X13). | Connect wetland route detours to B014-P12 and Z04 water-source screening. |
| Coastal & maritime | Rip currents require floating/swimming parallel to shore rather than fighting straight back (X14); tides trap hikers against headlands (X15); natural tsunami signs demand immediate high-ground evacuation (X16); storm surge requires early evacuation (X17); fastened USCG PFDs, float plans, marine warnings and staying with buoyant hulls save lives (X04–X06). | Maintain strict qualified rescue, maritime and environmental specialist review holds; never represent drafts as field-tested. |

Independent winter/ice rescue, desert medicine, mountain/avalanche, wetland, coastal-lifeguard and maritime safety specialists must review Z12 prior to field authorization.
