# Tools, cordage, construction and mechanical safety · evidence pack

Checked 2026-11-24. Hand tools, cordage, field repair, woodworking and mechanical principles across Upper Midwest and U.S. field contexts. Use `K01–K12` in the [claim register](claims.tsv) and dated primary references in the [source register](sources.tsv).

| Decision | Source-supported boundary | Editorial implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Tool selection and inspection | Match the tool to the task, inspect handles/heads/edges before use, sheath sharp blades in transit and keep cutting paths away from the body (K01–K03). | Require pre-use inspection, clear swing/work circles and immediate retirement of cracked handles or mushroomed striking heads (K02, K12). |
| Sharpening and maintenance | Secure blades firmly before filing/honing, keep fingers behind guards and preserve bevel geometry without overheating steel (K04). | Separate routine honing from damaged-steel retirement; forbid taping cracked axe/hatchet handles. |
| Cordage, knots and rigging limits | Knots, bends, moisture, UV and abrasion reduce rope strength sharply; improvised or camp cordage is NOT life-safety climbing gear (K09). | Explicitly prohibit unvalidated breaking-strength or working-load-limit claims for field-tied knots or natural fibers. |
| Woodworking, levers and mechanical advantage | Assess log bind and spring-pole tension before cutting (K05); simple machines trade force for distance with friction and material limits (K06); never fell standing snags in routine camp craft (K10, K11). | Reconcile historical B007, B007A, B008, B009 and B010 material while labeling all schematics conceptual and non-certified. |
| Field repairs and containers | Carry route-matched repair kits (K07) and recognize that temporary tape/splint repairs do not restore original storm or structural ratings. | Maintain strict food-grade and heat-rated container boundaries reconciled with Z04 (`W06`, `W13`). |

Independent mechanical, forestry-tool and rigging specialists must review mechanical-advantage schematics, saw-bind/spring-pole boundaries and knot/cordage limits prior to field authorization.
