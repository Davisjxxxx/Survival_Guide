# Disaster scenarios evidence pack (`disasters`, D01–D21)

The A02 [claim audit](../audit/a02/CLAIM_SUPPORT_AUDIT.csv) links exact sections, conditions, exclusions and specialist dependencies. The [technical findings](../audit/a02/TECHNICAL_FINDINGS.csv) record the changes made after P12–P15.

Grounds Zone `Z15` (Disaster Scenarios — 65 pages, preserving labeled reconstruction starter `Z15-R03`) in authoritative emergency-management and hazard guidance from FEMA/Ready.gov, NWS, US EPA/AirNow, CDC, and Minnesota Homeland Security and Emergency Management (HSEM).

- **Scope boundary:** Distinguishes official Watch vs Warning triggers, jurisdiction-specific evacuation orders, indoor clean-room filtration vs hazmat shelter-in-place, and structured 30-day household resilience.
- **Hard-stop exclusions:** Prohibits driving/walking into floodwater, sheltering under highway overpasses during tornadoes, generalized safety assurances, or unverified hazard predictions.
- **Specialist hold:** Requires qualified emergency management, meteorology, air-quality/public health, and accessibility specialist sign-off (`SPECIALIST_REVIEW_REGISTER.csv` H13 and P12–P15 additions).
