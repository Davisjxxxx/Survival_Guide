# V01 visual quality standard

**Visual audit and pilot · Publication HOLD**

Apply this standard to a rendered asset in its actual page context. A valid SVG or a unique geometry fingerprint is layout evidence. Accept the instruction only after reviewing the object, relationships, operating conditions and possible misreadings.

| Test | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|
| Purpose and hierarchy | One observable task or physical relationship. Put the primary object and decisive cue first. Remove dense caption duplication when the illustration adds no information. |
| Physical and anatomical accuracy | Show the relevant shape, connections, contact, orientation, material, state and forces. Show the error and the stop cue when relevant. Keep schematic uses separate from engineering drawings, installation or clinical qualification. |
| Comparison and scale | Match viewpoint and scale when comparing subjects. Include useful orientation or a measured scale when known. Clearly label conceptual, ideal and not-to-scale modes. |
| Diagnostic content | Keep specimen identity, accessions, life stage, locality scope, multiple views, missing features and lookalike requirements explicit. Expert validation remains a separate step. |
| Legibility | Check dark/light hierarchy and label-to-line contrast in color and grayscale. Inspect the actual SVG or image at a phone width and on the full Letter and A4 sheets. Keep accessible prose or alt text for information conveyed visually. |
| Rights and provenance | Record the original asset, relationship to displayed derivative and hash, license, credit, source, collection identification and expert review status. Clearly describe generated or staged imagery as illustration. |

## Dispositions

1. **Retain provisionally.** The current image serves the task in available checks. Record the depth and any specialist review still due.
2. **Retain with targeted correction.** Correct a label, hierarchy, stop state or boundary and inspect the revision.
3. **Replace with original technical illustration.** Build editable geometry for the actual mechanism or relationship. The nine V01 revisions sit here as produced candidates pending qualification.
4. **Replace with realistic instructional illustration.** Use reference-driven illustration for appearance or sequence. When using generated imagery, label it as illustration and verify details.
5. **Supplement with authentic documentary photography.** Record contextual limits and the situation photographed.
6. **Acquire authentic diagnostic specimen imagery.** Require identified and reviewed matched views, scale, locality or operating conditions, rights and expert disposition.
7. **Remove a generic hero candidate.** A figure that duplicates an accessible index can become compact text and links after owner review. Keep its editable source and preserve all source targets.

All 1,016 assets received layout and content triage. Selected rendered pages and representatives received visual inspection. Classes outside the sample are production dispositions. The geometry threshold from the previous backlog is a starting filter. Check each actual asset before approving a revision or removal. Retain color and digital grayscale samples for inspections. Physical print, contemporary browser assistive-device use and field trials have their own gates.
