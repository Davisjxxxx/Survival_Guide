- **Existing:** a 28-page original preparedness primer (3 PDFs, 10+9+9 pages); a research and production blueprint proposing approximately 870 pages; a dated Astra CLI handoff that defines 16 zones, schema and safety gates.

## Delivery Target

- An original, source-traceable, region-aware, offline-first survival encyclopedia consisting of **16 zone PDFs plus one combined indexed master PDF** and editable authoring files.
- Planning ranges: **800–1,100 pages**; **150–200 field-identification profiles**; **180–250 technical diagrams**; **350–600 real, permission-cleared diagnostic or habitat photographs**. Targets change based on real content/rights feasibility.
- Each procedure answers: **WHAT | WHERE | WHEN | IDENTIFY | MATERIALS | STEPS | VERIFY | ABORT / DANGER | REGION/LAW | SOURCES**. Concise language must never omit a critical step.
- No AI-generated botanical/fungal/mineral photos as identification evidence; high-risk topics require domain expert review.

## Ownership

**ChatGPT / GPT-6 High or Extra High:** substantive research, benchmark comparison, current references, evidence and claim IDs, field methods, species and material research, concise instructional text, diagram specifications, photo inspection/rights decisions, editorial and safety triage, original sample PDFs, content approval. Can produce substantive verified chapters and downloadable files in active chats, even with no CLI.

**Astra CLI:** local repository audit, content schema and validation tooling, asset ingest/cataloging, layout/illustration production from approved specs, reproducible PDFs, cross-reference and indexes, render QA, build tests, Git run records, distribution packaging. May not invent field facts or silently rewrite approved source text.

**Human specialists:** positive high-risk fungus/plant identification and toxic species review, medical safety review, licensed/qualified electrical and construction review, food preservation verification as needed. ChatGPT cannot replace qualified verification or physical test results.

**User:** priorities, decision at each major gate, local Astra invocation, real-world test and final release authorization.

## Definition of Done / Proof Gates

1. **A (Repo + tooling):** paths, branch/commit, no-overwrite audit, build commands, source/claim/photo registers, working dummy PDF, automated validation, run record.
2. **B (Evidence):** high-consequence claims linked to dated source IDs; unsupported practices quarantined.
3. **C (Field pilot):** sample procedures include materials, repeatable steps, checks, warnings, sources and phone/print legibility.
4. **D (Identification atlas):** real diagnostic photos with provenance, taxon identity, habitat, lookalikes, restrictions and review; photos alone never establish edible safety.
5. **E (Regional release):** Midwest edition is safety reviewed with no unresolved critical/high defects.
6. **F (Complete alpha):** 16 zones content-complete, editorially reviewed and cross-referenced.
7. **G (External review):** qualified high-consequence review and tracked corrections, no open critical/high defects.
8. **H (Publication):** reproducible print/mobile PDFs, legally cleared images, citations, glossary/index, accessibility and explicit authorization.

## Main Risks / Controls

| Risk | Impact | Required mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Toxic mushroom/plant misidentification | Severe injury/death | Qualified independent species/edibility review; source-verified identification plates; uncertainty flags |
| Incorrect water/medical/CO/food preservation procedure | Severe injury/death | Current primary evidence; calculations; specialist review; fail-closed publication |
| Copyright or image rights gaps | Delayed/illegal distribution | Individual image licenses and attribution ledger; exclude unlicensed imagery |
| Excessive scope and polished filler | Large but unusable book | Standard short field-card format, required checks, incremental acceptance |
| Absent local repo evidence | Unknown implementation state | First Astra run must inventory, not modify blindly |
| Outdated regional legal rules | Wrong hunting/trapping guidance | Jurisdiction and 'as of' dates for regulations; scheduled review before release |

## Control Rule

Nothing advances from draft to verified/approved/published without real evidence and the appropriate review. An unseen repo or missing image manifest is **unverified**, not 'probably complete.' The ChatGPT research/editorial layer owns final wording and technical claims, Astra owns production engineering, and domain specialists own independent high-risk verification.
