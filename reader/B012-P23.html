No container, no fire: limits and choices

When you lack a heat source, vessel, suitable treatment and known safe water, there may be no reliable rapid drinking-water solution from the landscape.

NO GEARNo reliable no-gear treatment is promised; shelter and rescue are central.
FOUND MATERIALA clean recovered container with a suitable, verified treatment may be useful; unknown materials are not evidence.
EQUIPPEDChoose validated filter plus disinfection, or a safe boiling setup, matched to the water source.
IN THE FIELD · FOUR ACTIONS
01Look for known safe water

Check carried supplies, safe sealed water, nearby buildings or a vehicle without risking travel into whiteout or weak ice.

02Check available treatment

A correctly rated device or labeled disinfectant may help only for its specified contaminants and conditions.

03Reject false shortcuts

Sand/moss clarification, melted snow and a clear stream do not establish microbial safety.

04Escalate early

Protect warmth and signal for assistance. Reassess risk rather than attempting uncertain ice travel or improvised boiling.
