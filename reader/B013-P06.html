B013 · MINNESOTA ICE SAFETY · P06 · SELF-RESCUE

IF YOU BREAK THROUGH

Turn back, kick horizontal, pull out, roll away

STAGED SCENE • NOT A RELIABLE ICE-CONDITION IDENTIFICATION PHOTO
Cold shock can disrupt breathing immediately. Keeping your airway above water and escaping promptly are the first priorities.

HOW THE DECISION OR ACTION WORKS

  1. Control breathing; keep clothing on — Do not waste time stripping winter layers in the water.
  2. Face the direction you came — Place arms on unbroken ice; use picks if available.
  3. Kick to become horizontal — Drive forward while pulling your torso onto the ice.
  4. Lie flat and roll away — Return toward your approach path. Seek warm shelter immediately.

CHECK BEFORE CONTINUING

  • Clear of the weak edge before standing.
  • 911/medical help initiated as soon as possible.

WRONG SIGNAL → CORRECTION

Cannot lift onto the iceKeep airway up; call for help and continue controlled attempts if possible.
Ice breaks againFollow the approach direction; stay spread flat.
Exhaustion or reduced movementUrgent rescue is needed; cold-water incapacitation can develop rapidly.
STOP CONDITION: If ice strength cannot be verified, stay off. If someone has fallen through, summon emergency responders immediately.
