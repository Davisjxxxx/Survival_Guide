B012 · MINNESOTA WINTER SURVIVALCLOTHING SYSTEM • MANAGE SWEAT · P05

ZERO EQUIPMENT FIRST · FIELD DECISION

Vent While Working. Seal When Resting.

Moisture from sweat can strip away useful insulation in minutes.

AT A GLANCE

  • Reduce effort before heavy sweating starts.
  • Control openings; do not expose skin unnecessarily.
  • Close layers and add insulation before stopping.

THE FOUR ACTIONS

1

BEFORE WORK

Check hands, face and feet; loosen a neck opening or reduce a removable layer.

2

DURING WORK

Use a sustainable pace; pause early if dampness forms on inner clothing.

3

WHEN STOPPING

Immediately close openings, cover extremities and shelter from wind.

4

IF CLOTHES ARE WET

Seek sheltered, warmer conditions; replace saturated layers only when safe dry insulation is ready.

RECOGNIZE • CHOOSE • ESCALATE

IF YOU HAVE MORE / LESS

01 · NO EQUIPMENT

Slow down; regulate collar and cuffs; use terrain to get out of wind before resting.

02 · IMPROVISED

Add salvaged dry fabric loosely over existing clothing; keep absorbent material dry.

03 · EQUIPPED

Adjust wicking base, insulation and wind shell as separate layers; use ventilating zips.

WHEN IT GOES WRONG

Overheating while building shelterReduce effort and ventilate before sweat soaks the layer against skin.
Removing wet clothing in the openMove to wind protection first; avoid prolonged uncovered exposure.
STOP / ESCALATE: If clumsy, confused, excessively drowsy or unusually unable to assist yourself: stop exertion and activate emergency help.
