P21 • B012 WINTER SURVIVALZERO-IGNITION FALLBACK

No fire: keep heat, stay findable

If fire is unavailable, lower heat loss before wasting more time and energy. Insulation, wind protection and rescue signaling become the working system.

NO GEARChoose a wind-protected site, get up off cold ground, stay dry and signal.
FOUND MATERIALUse dry fabric, salvage, insulated vehicle/building or safe recovery materials if available.
EQUIPPEDUse emergency beacon, dry sleeping insulation and shelter designed for the conditions.
IN THE FIELD · FOUR ACTIONS
01Get out of moving air

Use safer terrain or a simple windbreak, avoiding unstable trees, frozen waterways and burial hazards.

02Insulate beneath you

Build a dry barrier between the body and frozen ground; keep wet debris away from skin.

03Manage moisture

Reduce exertion; adjust clothing to limit sweat. Use dry layers or salvage when possible.

04Signal and monitor

Seek rescue early when possible. Watch for confusion, fumbling, slurred speech or severe drowsiness.

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS
FAILURE → CAUSE → CORRECTION
Work causes sweatingExcessive exertionReduce activity, adjust layers and protect dry insulation.
Wind enters beddingIncomplete barrierBlock drafts without sealing breathing space.
Poor coordination/confusionPossible hypothermiaTreat as a medical emergency; seek help and protect from further cooling.
STOP / CHANGE COURSE: A natural bed is not proof of overnight survival. Do not use unvented fuel-burning heat inside enclosed shelters.
