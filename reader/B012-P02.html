ZERO EQUIPMENT FIRST · FIELD DECISION

Know Your Situation Before You Move

Lost, wet, injured, or stranded: the first move is not always walking.

AT A GLANCE

  • Stop if visibility or judgment is declining.
  • Get out of wind; preserve a known safe refuge.
  • Call for help and share a location when possible.

THE FOUR ACTIONS

1

STOP AND COUNT

Who is present? Who is wet, injured, disoriented or exhausted?

2

CHOOSE REFUGE

A safe vehicle or building generally beats unplanned travel; keep exits and air clear.

3

SET A SIGNAL

Use phone, whistle, bright clothing or a large ground marker; avoid risky travel to signal.

4

REASSESS

Move only if staying poses a greater hazard or a known safe destination is reachable.

RECOGNIZE • CHOOSE • ESCALATE

IF YOU HAVE MORE / LESS

01 · NO EQUIPMENT

Stay together if safe; choose wind-protected dry ground; use a known route only when objectively safer.

02 · IMPROVISED

Bright clothing, salvage, tracks, or an existing windbreak help visibility and insulation.

03 · EQUIPPED

Call 911 / use satellite SOS, share coordinates, activate safe vehicle hazard signals.

WHEN IT GOES WRONG

Leaving a safe vehicle in a whiteoutStay in or close to the safe vehicle; call rescue, conserve heat and be visible.
Walking toward an uncertain landmarkStop. Seek shelter, relocate only from a verified route and destination.
STOP / ESCALATE: If a vehicle is unsafe (water, unstable ice, fire, traffic), leave for the nearest safe location. Never idle a vehicle with a blocked tailpipe.
