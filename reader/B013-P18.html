B013 · MINNESOTA ICE SAFETY · P18 · EMERGENCY DESK REFERENCE

AFTER A BREAKTHROUGH: ACT, DO NOT GUESS

A compressed reminder for emergencies, not a substitute for rescue training.

ORIGINAL EMERGENCY FLOW · TRAINING REVIEW REQUIRED
Call 911 immediately. Report the lake, exact land access, number of people, and whether anyone remains in the water.

Actions by role

•
If you witness the incidentCall 911 first. Stay on stable land. Offer reach/throw assistance only if possible without approaching weak ice.
•
If you are in the waterControl breathing as able. Face the direction you came from. Get your body horizontal, kick and pull toward that route.
•
Once outLie flat and roll or crawl away from the hole on the approach path. Move to shelter; prevent further heat loss.
•
After rescue or escapeReplace wet items with dry insulation when possible, insulate from the ground, protect from wind and seek emergency medical assessment.

CHECK BEFORE CONTINUING

  • Emergency dispatcher has precise lake/shore access information.
  • No bystander approaches an uncertain hole or makes a second victim.
  • Cold-water exposure is treated as a medical emergency.

WRONG SIGNAL → CORRECTION

Jump in to pull them outDo not enter weak ice or water without professional rescue training.
Stand immediately after exitingRemain low and move away from the hole on your entry route.
Aggressively warm limbsAvoid rapid direct extremity heating and rough handling; follow emergency guidance.
STOP: A person in the water, altered consciousness, breathing difficulty, or significant cold exposure requires emergency response.
