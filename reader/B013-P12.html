B013 · MINNESOTA ICE SAFETY · P12 · FAMILY / PETS

KEEP CHILDREN & PETS ON LAND

Prevent a rescue emergency instead of responding to one.

STAGED ILLUSTRATION • NOT A FIELD-VERIFIED ICE OR RESCUE PHOTOGRAPH
Children and animals must not approach unknown ice to investigate or retrieve objects.

HOW THE DECISION OR ACTION WORKS

  1. Establish a shore boundary — Keep children close and dogs leashed where frozen water is accessible.
  2. Explain the hazard — A snow-covered pond, stream or ditch may look like ground.
  3. Do not chase — If a pet goes onto the ice, do not follow onto unknown ice.
  4. Call for help — Call 911 when a person is at risk or in the water; keep bystanders on land.

CHECK BEFORE CONTINUING

  • Kids and pets stay on firm land away from edges.
  • A responsible adult maintains continuous supervision.
  • There is no ice-edge approach for a dropped toy or runaway dog.

WRONG SIGNAL → CORRECTION

Dog walks acrossAnimal weight cannot establish a human-safe route.
Ice looks like a snow fieldHidden water and moving currents remain possible.
Rescuer runs after petCall trained responders; do not create a second victim.
STOP CONDITION: No one steps onto unknown ice for a pet, toy or shortcut.
