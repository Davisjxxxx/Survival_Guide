SURVIVAL FIELD ENCYCLOPEDIAB012 / P01 · RESEARCH PROOF
FIRST RESPONSE

STOP. STABILIZE. SIGNAL.

If lost or exposed in winter, protect judgment and body temperature before consuming energy on a complex build.

Staged illustration, not a real incident photograph.
Do this — no extra equipment
  1. Stop in a safer spot; avoid exposed ice and other immediate hazards.
  2. Check injuries, wet clothing, wind direction, daylight, and available shelter.
  3. Cover exposed skin and reduce wind exposure using what you have.
  4. Insulate yourself from snow or frozen ground and signal for help when possible.
VERIFY
You can still think clearly, move safely, and describe your location.
FAILURE
Continuing into low visibility or spending energy on unnecessary travel.
CORRECT
Stabilize at a recognizable, safer location; communicate or signal.
STOP: Confusion, fumbling, slurred speech, or unusual drowsiness: suspected hypothermia; seek emergency medical help.UPGRADE: Phone/beacon; protective outer layers and insulation.
