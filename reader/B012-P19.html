Winter fire: decide before you ignite

A controlled outdoor fire can supply heat and a signal. Do not rely on one you cannot ignite, fuel, supervise and extinguish safely.

NO GEARA reliable fire may be impossible. Prioritize wind shelter and dry insulation; friction methods are conditional.
FOUND MATERIALA usable lighter, clean metal vessel or dry fuel may change the decision; inspect each item.
EQUIPPEDUse approved fire site and ignition equipment; operate fuel-burning stoves only as directed outdoors.
IN THE FIELD · FOUR ACTIONS
01Inspect the site

Reject roots, duff, dry brush, overhead limbs and shelter proximity. Never assume snow makes the site safe.

02Stage dry fuel

Set aside small dry tinder, kindling and larger deadwood before ignition. Keep reserves off wet snow.

03Ignite only when feasible

Use a dependable source if available; primitive friction ignition needs dry parts, time and practice.

04Control and close

Keep the fire small, monitor wind and nearby fuels; extinguish completely until cold.

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS
FAILURE → CAUSE → CORRECTION

STOP / CHANGE COURSE: Never operate open flame or a camp stove inside an improvised enclosed shelter. If fire effort makes you wet, exhausted or less sheltered, change priorities.
