## Design gate per image or graphic

1. **Real-image need:** Real identification/diagnostic or measured-test claims require authentic evidence, not synthetic scenes.
2. **Concept-only illustration:** Original AI illustration or vector is allowed for non-diagnostic concepts when labeled STAGED / CONCEPTUAL.
3. **Mechanism accuracy:** Independent subject expert verifies arrows, geometry, scale implication, hand positions, materials, sequence and stop conditions.
4. **Rights:** No distribution until license/permission and attribution are recorded for the specific image, not merely its source URL.
5. **Safe editorial use:** Preview image cannot silently authorize untrained excavation, indoor fire, ice crossings or unsafe medical care.
