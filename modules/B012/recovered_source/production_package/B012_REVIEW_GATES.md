## Field-test records to design before tests (not conducted)

For each physical shelter or fire/water process, capture: method ID, weather and actual temperature, precipitation, wind, substrate/snow condition, materials dry/wet condition, built dimensions, tools, duration, safety monitoring, controlled outcome, failure, evacuation/abort, videos, reviewer signature, and any restrictions. Never expose volunteers to avoidable cold-injury or enclosed combustion risk for a photo shoot.

## Reader page types

- Decision: two or three-tier solution matrix with safe refusal path.
- Build: hero photograph + staged step crops + connection diagram + failure pair.
- Recognition: real photos with diagnostic identifiers and uncertainty warnings.
- Medical: real medically reviewed recognition imagery + concise emergency triage; no AI lesion image as evidence.
- Failure: real failed scenario (controlled) paired with verified correction and abort trigger.
