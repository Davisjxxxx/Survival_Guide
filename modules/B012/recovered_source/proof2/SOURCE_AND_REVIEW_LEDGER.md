# B012-2 editorial evidence / pending gates

**Version:** 2026-11-22. **Scope:** B012 P13–P18, draft visual decision plates. No approved publication content, real photos, measured snow strength, or construction testing.

## Primary references consulted

1. National Weather Service, Winter Storm: During the Storm — https://www.weather.gov/safety/winter-during — shelter and staying dry guidance; broad emergency options, not tested roof specifications.
2. CDC, Preventing Hypothermia — https://www.cdc.gov/winter-weather/prevention/index.html — warning signs and wet-weather risk.
3. CDC, Preventing Frostbite — https://www.cdc.gov/winter-weather/prevention/preventing-frostbite.html — symptoms and avoidance of direct heating.
4. NOAA/NWS, Winter Preparedness — https://www.weather.gov/fsd/winter_preparedness — stay put, windbreak, avoid eating snow, sheltered vehicle.

## Specific reservations

- P13 natural winter A-frame: no measured support spacing, snow capacity or shelter temperature. Framing is a concept requiring a hands-on structural proof.
- P14 lean-to + outdoor fire: **no** claim that a rock ring or a nominal separation makes sparks, smoke or CO safe; local fire restrictions are time-varying.
- P15 snow trench: no stepwise roof assembly or snow thickness numbers until specialist review and tests.
- P16 snow cave: hazard recognition only; no excavation/entry instructions. Snowpack, terrain, drift and egress hazards require trained evaluation.
- P17 partially excavated / stone roof: intentionally blocked as unengineered emergency build; no enclosed fire.
- P18 moisture: dry and ventilated does not equal adequate thermal performance.
