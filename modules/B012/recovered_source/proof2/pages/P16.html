Snow cave / quinzhee: advanced only

Snow walls are a structural system, not just insulation

CONCEPT / CUTAWAY

Original schematic. Not a measured or field-tested structure.

Materials and upgrades

No gear: use a safer natural windbreak if possible.

Improvised: found digging tools do not establish safe roof strength.

Equipped: qualified cold-weather/snow-specialist supervision.

ACTION / DECISION SEQUENCE

  1. Do not excavate overhead snow without training and appropriate stability checks.
  2. Exclude avalanche slopes, cornices, drift-prone entrances, and unstable snow.
  3. Keep an exit and ventilation concept in view; do not burn fuel in the cave.
  4. Retreat from cracking, settling, drifting, oxygen/ventilation concerns, or excessive fatigue.
