P13 | B012 WINTER SURVIVAL

Natural winter A-frame

Conditional no-gear roof, not a snow-rated shelter

CONCEPT / CUTAWAY

Original schematic. Not a measured or field-tested structure.

Materials and upgrades

No gear: deadfall with dry brush and debris, if abundant and sound.

Improvised: salvaged sheet or cord helps secure the frame.

Equipped: purpose-designed winter shelter, installed as specified.

ACTION / DECISION SEQUENCE

  1. Choose stable, sheltered ground; reject hanging limbs, runoff channels, and snow-laden trees.
  2. Use only sound fallen material. Lay out supports before placing any overhead load.
  3. Arrange light ribs so an overlapping covering can shed precipitation; keep wet material off the sleeper.
  4. Check stability and exit before occupying. If the structure shifts or loads with snow, move away.
