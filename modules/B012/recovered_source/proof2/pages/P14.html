P14 | B012 WINTER SURVIVAL

Lean-to and outdoor fire separation

Windbreak does not make enclosed combustion safe

CONCEPT / CUTAWAY

Original schematic. Not a measured or field-tested structure.

Materials and upgrades

No gear: terrain windshadow and sound deadfall; fire is optional.

Improvised: recovered windproof fabric if it can be fixed safely.

Equipped: approved fire site and rated shelter where available.

ACTION / DECISION SEQUENCE

  1. Put an open windbreak between you and prevailing wind, not beneath dead branches.
  2. Use sound, securely supported fallen wood; keep an exit unobstructed.
  3. Treat an outdoor fire as a separate activity: check local restrictions and site hazards.
  4. If smoke, sparks, snow melt, or shifting wind threaten the shelter, extinguish or relocate the fire.
